# Ecommerce Business Overview

## Database Overview
The ecommerce database contains 40,000 orders from 10,000 registered customers. The available order data covers the period from March 16, 2026, to June 14, 2026, providing an overview of customer activity, order statuses, payment outcomes, and the product catalogue during this period.

## Customer Activity
Out of 10,000 registered customers, 8,800 customers placed at least one order, while 1,200 customers had no recorded purchases. Among the 8,800 customers who made purchases, 3,801 were repeat customers, representing a repeat purchase rate of 43.19%. The remaining 4,999 customers placed only one order. This shows that the dataset includes both returning and one-time customers.

## Order and Payment Status
The order data shows that 19,979 orders were delivered and 8,113 were shipped. Other orders were recorded under statuses such as paid, packed, cancelled, and placed. Separately, 37,822 orders had a paid payment status, while 2,178 had failed payments. All 2,178 failed-payment records were associated with cancelled orders. Order status and payment status represent different aspects of the transaction and should therefore be analysed separately.

## Product Catalogue
The catalogue contains 4,000 products associated with 120 brands and 18 categories. The category structure consists of four parent categories and 14 subcategories. Products are assigned directly to subcategories rather than parent categories. Regarding product availability, 3,867 products (96.68%) are active, while 133 products (3.32%) are inactive.

## Overall Observations and Limitations
The dataset provides a useful overview of customer purchasing activity, order processing, payment outcomes, and catalogue structure. It also identifies areas for further analysis, such as repeat purchasing behaviour and inactive products. However, the findings are based on approximately three months of data and should not be treated as evidence of long-term business trends.
