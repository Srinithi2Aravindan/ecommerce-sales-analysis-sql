# Milestone 4: Customer Segmentation & Marketing Insights

## 1. The Customer Retention Reality

The ecommerce dataset contains 8,438 purchasing customers during the observation period (March 16 – June 14, 2026). A repeat customer is defined as someone who completed at least two paid orders during this period.

Of these customers, 4,679 (55.45%) made only one purchase, while 3,759 (44.55%) returned for additional purchases.

Although repeat customers represent less than half of the purchasing customer base, they generated ₹24.79 crore, contributing 87.63% of total paid revenue. One-time customers contributed only ₹3.50 crore (12.37%).

Average observed spending per repeat customer was ₹65,938.72, compared with ₹7,479.28 for one-time customers.

## 2. Customer Segment Breakdown

Customers were classified according to paid-order frequency:

| Segment          | Customers | Customer Share | Revenue Contribution |
|------------------|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| One-time         |  4,679    | 55.45%         |     12.37%           |
| Occasional (2–5) |   1,697   | 20.11%         |         16.16%       |
| Regular (6–10)   |   1,391   | 16.48%         |        26.55%        |
| Frequent (11–20) |   433     | 5.13%          |        16.09%        |
| VIP (21+)        |   238     | 2.82%          |        28.83%        |

VIP customers represent the most valuable frequency-based segment. These 238 customers generated ₹8.16 crore in revenue, accounting for 28.83% of total paid revenue.

Their average purchase frequency was 45.19 orders, average customer spending was ₹3,42,674.64, and average order value was ₹7,582.43.

Regular customers are another important segment, contributing 26.55% of revenue. Together, VIP and Regular customers generate 55.38% of total revenue.

## 3. Q4 Marketing Recommendations

Marketing should prioritize customer retention while improving conversion among existing one-time buyers.

- **VIP customers:** Introduce loyalty rewards, exclusive offers and personalized retention campaigns.
- **Regular customers:** Encourage continued engagement through personalized recommendations and loyalty incentives.
- **Frequent customers:** Protect purchasing behaviour through targeted offers and relevant product recommendations.
- **Occasional customers:** Encourage progression towards regular purchasing through timely promotional campaigns.
- **One-time customers:** Prioritize second-purchase email campaigns, personalized discounts and product recommendations.

Rather than allocating the entire budget towards new customer acquisition, Q4 spending should give greater attention to retention and repeat-purchase conversion.

Campaign effectiveness should be measured using repeat purchase rate, customer revenue, average order value and conversion to second purchase.

## 4. Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that repeat customers are the primary revenue contributors. Although one-time buyers form 55.45% of purchasing customers, they contribute only 12.37% of revenue.

The recommended strategy is to protect high-value VIP and Regular customers while converting One-time and Occasional buyers into repeat purchasers.
